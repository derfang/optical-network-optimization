Key Structural Takeaways:
1. Constraint Stability: In the proposed CA (LP-CA), the number of constraints is strictly bounded by |D| + |E|*|S| (stabilizing at 572 constraints regardless of k).
2. Combinatorial Growth in SSA & SA: In SSA, pairwise path-sharing constraints explode to 2,790 constraints (11.1x growth). In SA, contiguity constraints explode to 2,412 constraints.
3. Relaxed Lower Bound: Solves in 3 - 34 ms, verifying the theoretical lower bound.
