Observation: Pre-computed channel sets eliminate non-linear and Big-M constraints
for slot contiguity entirely, enabling standard binary linear constraints on link capacity.

Key Takeaways:
1. SSA suffers from weak Big-M continuous relaxations that degrade branch-and-bound pruning.
2. SA suffers from high constraint density due to slot-transition contiguity conditions.
3. LP-CA achieves the fastest and most stable scaling as k increases (1.33s at k=4 vs. 3.83s for SA).
4. Relaxed RSA provides a rapid lower-bound benchmark within 10-100 ms.
