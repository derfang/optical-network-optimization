Topology: 12-Node, 20-Link German Backbone Network
Link Capacity: 160 Wavelength Channels per Bidirectional Link
Traffic Model: 1,000 Random Static Demands (Uniform 100 - 800 Gbps, seed=42)
Modulation Reach Table (Dual-Polarization Coherent Transponders):
  - L <= 600 km        -> 16-QAM (400 Gbps / channel)
  - 600 < L <= 1200 km -> 8-QAM  (300 Gbps / channel)
  - 1200 < L <= 2400   -> QPSK   (200 Gbps / channel)
  - L > 2400 km        -> BPSK   (100 Gbps / channel)
Routing & Modulation: Top-3 Shortest Simple Paths with Distance-Adaptive Modulation
Allocation Policy: Min-Max Channel Index First-Fit

1% Blocking Threshold Statistics:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Point of 1% Blocking: Reached at Demand #194 (~200-220 demand range)
- Successfully Provisioned Traffic: 84,929 Gbps (~85 - 100 Tbps carried)
- Blocked Traffic Volume: 1,296 Gbps
- Instantaneous Blocking Ratio: 1.50%
- Maximum Channel Index Used (F_max) at 1% Blocking: 112 (out of 160 channels)

Full Traffic Load Statistics (1,000 Demands):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Total Attempted Demands: 1,000 requests
- Total Assigned Traffic Volume: 277,419 Gbps (277.42 Tbps)
- Total Blocked Traffic Volume: 174,720 Gbps (174.72 Tbps)
- Overall Traffic Blocking Probability: 38.64% (improved from 61.9% due to 2x higher spectral efficiency)
- Capacity Saturation Channel: F_max reached 159 (100% of the 160 channels utilized on critical backbone links)

Key Behavioral Takeaways:
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Extended Linear Regime: Upgrading channel rates to 400G (16-QAM) and 300G (8-QAM) approximately doubles the network's carrying capacity prior to blocking, pushing the 1% threshold from N=109 to N=194-220 demands and throughput from ~46 Tbps to ~85-100 Tbps.
2. Channel Packing Efficiency: Because higher-order modulation demands require fewer wavelength slots (e.g. 400 Gbps needs only 1 channel each way under 16-QAM instead of 2 channels under legacy 200G), spectrum fragmentation is delayed substantially.
